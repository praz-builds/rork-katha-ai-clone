You have a story in you. Katha helps you get it out of your head and onto the page, then gives it a cover, a voice and readers.

Start with a single idea. Pick a genre, bring a character or make one, and Katha writes the first chapter while you watch the first page arrive. You steer from there, one chapter at a time.

WRITE WITH AI, YOUR WAY
• Twelve genres: adventure, comedy, educational, fanfiction, folktale, historical, sci-fi, fantasy, mystery, horror, slice of life and romance
• Start from a one-line idea, and choose the length: a short read or a longer series
• Every new story comes with its own cover art
• Craft characters with a look, a history and an illustrated portrait, then save them and cast them in your next story
• At the end of each chapter, choose where the story goes next, or re-prompt a chapter to try it another way
• Edit any line by hand, for free, as often as you like
• Download a story as a PDF

READ, FREE
• Reading is free and unlimited
• Katha Originals, our house collection, plus stories published by other writers
• Explore by genre and author, star stories to your library and pick up where you left off
• Comment on stories, like them and follow the writers you enjoy
• Keep a daily reading streak going

LISTEN
• Turn any chapter into an audio narration with a natural AI voice, and keep it for good
• Genre music plays softly while you read, and one tap mutes it

PRIVATE UNTIL YOU SAY SO
Everything you write starts private. Publishing is one toggle, and only you decide. You can report a story or comment that breaks the rules, and block its author, from the story page or the reader.

YOUR PHOTOS, YOUR CHOICE
You can attach a photo from your library as a style reference for a character portrait. It is sent only to draw that one image and is never stored on our servers. The portrait is always original art, never a copy of a real face.

HOW CREDITS WORK
Reading is always free. Writing chapters, character images beyond the free ones, and narration use credits. New accounts get free character images and starter credits, and you can earn more through your reading streak, feedback and invites. An optional Katha subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly) adds credits every period, and credit packs are available. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Katha is for adults 18 and over. Some genres, such as romance and horror, include mature themes.
• Stories are currently written in English.
• Stories, images and narration are generated by AI. AI can get things wrong, so read before you publish.
• Sign-in is by email with a one-time code. No password.
